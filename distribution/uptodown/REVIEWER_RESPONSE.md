Kalima is an original native Android application written in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, with its own Quranic word corpus, study scheduler, quiz engine, lock-screen flow, and offline reader. Kalima never unlocks the device or bypasses a PIN, password, pattern, fingerprint, or face authentication.
