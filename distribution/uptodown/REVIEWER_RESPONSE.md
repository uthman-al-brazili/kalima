Thank you for reviewing Kalima and for explaining that the previous submission looked too similar to an unmodified template. We have replaced that submission with version 0.30.3 and updated the descriptions and localized artwork to show Kalima’s distinctive implemented workflows. Kalima is an original native Android application written in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, with its own Quranic word corpus, study scheduler, quiz engine, lock-screen flow, widget, and offline reader. Kalima’s primary experience is optional lock-screen learning for Quranic Arabic in English and Brazilian Portuguese. When enabled by the user, a short study card or quiz can appear over the still-locked keyguard when the display turns on or when the user returns to the phone. Kalima never unlocks the device or bypasses a PIN, password, pattern, fingerprint, or face authentication.

Version 0.30.3 makes that purpose clearer in onboarding and Study, and adds a distinctive Daily Quran Word home-screen widget with centered Arabic learning content, a subtle deep-green gradient, transliteration, contextual meaning, Quran reference, a Next action that does not change learning progress, and an exact direct path into the same word in Kalima. The app also includes a 42,117-card offline Quran corpus, contextual verse quizzes, spaced repetition, Arabic foundations, human Quran recordings, and offline Quran reading.

The new public images focus on the real lock-screen lesson, lock-screen quiz, Daily Quran Word widget, and word-to-Quran-context reader instead of generic template screens. Lock-screen learning remains opt-in, the app contains no ads, account, or analytics, and learning progress stays local. Please consider the updated APK and materials for review.
